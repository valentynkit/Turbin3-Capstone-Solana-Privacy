## Correction to the brief
"Not payroll" was argued on "employer already knows the employee". Wrong frame: stealth hides the employee from the public (that wallet W gets paid by X monthly, and W's whole income history), not from the employer. Payroll wants both halves. The objection is engineering cost of batching and proof generation, not chain fees. Reclassify: payroll = phase-two target (needs batch tooling); launch use = one-off payments (grants, bounties, invoices); target buyers = stablecoin payroll and B2B settlement platforms.

## Target market statement [medium]
Stablecoin-payroll and B2B settlement platforms serving regulated or reputation-sensitive payers, plus commerce platforms wanting merchant revenue hidden from competitors but readable by regulators. Pain point validated by a named non-Solana analogue (Toku/Aleo/Paxos) and Solana vendor positioning; no Solana-native named customer yet; DAO/grant payouts unverified.
