# Compliance posture research (2026-09-11)

Method: primary fetches (EUR-Lex, govinfo, Cornell LII, Treasury, solana-program docs). FATF site blocked; FATF claims from memory, unverified.

## Findings
- **Auditor key is per-mint, global, set by the mint's confidential-transfer authority** (solana-program.com/docs/confidential-balances). For USDC that is Circle. No per-account viewing keys at protocol level. Each account's own ElGamal key "can be shared among multiple users (e.g. regulators)" voluntarily.
  → In our design every one-time account's key is derived from the shared secret, so **sender or recipient can disclose a single payment's key** to an auditor: per-payment selective disclosure without issuer cooperation. Mint auditor is a bonus when the issuer sets one, not a requirement.
- **EU TFR 2023/1113** Art. 2(4): P2P transfers without a CASP are out of scope. Art. 14: originator/beneficiary data travels CASP-to-CASP off-chain; a fresh on-chain address changes nothing there. Art. 14(5): >€1,000 to self-hosted requires CASP to assess ownership; harder against single-use addresses; no guidance. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32023R1113
- **US BSA 31 CFR 1010.410(f)**: institution-to-institution, $3,000 threshold. https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/31/1010.410
- **Tornado Cash**: 2022 designation reasoning = pooling breaking sender↔recipient link, DPRK laundering. 2025 delisting procedural (Van Loon), Treasury still hostile to mixers. https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0057
- **FinCEN CVC mixing NPRM (Oct 2023)**: "obfuscates the source, destination, or amount"; illustrative prong 4: "creating and using single-use wallets, addresses, or accounts." Disjunctive definition; literal reading could sweep in stealth addresses. Counter: source public, no pool, amounts auditor-recoverable. No guidance drawing that line; finalization status unverified. https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2023-10-23/html/2023-23449.htm — **biggest textual exposure**.
- **MiCA 2023/1114** Recital 22: fully decentralised, no issuer → outside Titles II–IV; CASPs servicing it remain regulated. Art. 76(3): trading platforms may not admit assets with "inbuilt anonymisation function unless the holders … and their transaction history can be identified by the CASP" → listable only if the CASP can obtain decrypt access. No ESMA/EBA guidance on auditable-privacy designs. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32023R1114
- Solana Foundation framing: "auditing rights to ensure regulatory compliance" (solana.com/solutions/token-extensions); Helius blog on institutional monitoring. No MiCA-specific SF statement found.

## LOI-ready paragraph (condensed)
No pool, no anonymity set, visible sender, ordinary tokens throughout. Travel-rule duties bind VASPs, not protocols; self-hosted P2P is out of TFR scope by text [high]. Identity data travels off-chain between VASPs regardless of on-chain address [high]. Open exposure: FinCEN's proposed mixing definition names single-use addresses [medium; rule status unverified]. MiCA: issuer-less protocol outside Titles II–IV; listability turns on CASP decrypt access [medium]. Audit access: per-payment key disclosure by either party; mint auditor only where the issuer sets one [high]. Considered legal-engineering position, not a cleared opinion.
