Confidential payouts on Solana

A visible payer pays many people. The public sees neither who got paid nor how much. An auditor key keeps it usable by real businesses.

  1. Publish. A recipient who wants to be unlinkable shares one address, once. It never receives funds directly. A recipient who is fine with a fixed address just has a confidential account.
  2. Pay. The payer runs one payout to everyone. For stealth recipients it derives a fresh account nobody can link back to them and pays into it; for plain recipients it transfers directly. Amounts are encrypted either way. Recipients can be offline.
  3. Find and sweep. A stealth recipient scans, spots their accounts, signs with the one-time key, and moves the money into a fresh account of their own. The fee comes from a little SOL the payer left behind, so their main wallet never appears.
  4. Audit. The mint's auditor key can read every amount, and either party can hand over the key for a single payment. Nobody can spend with either.

What we build: the two on-chain pieces (address book, one-time accounts) and a batch payout client. What we reuse: Solana's own confidential balances and proof system. Not building: a wallet, a web app, a pool, or our own cryptography primitives.
