  1. Publish. The recipient shares one address, once. It never receives funds directly.
  2. Pay. The sender uses that address to derive a fresh account nobody can link back to the recipient, and pays into it with the amount encrypted. The recipient can be offline.
  3. Find and sweep. The recipient scans, spots their accounts, proves ownership, and moves the money out.
  4. Audit. A designated auditor key can read amounts. Nobody else can. Nobody can spend with it.

What we build: the two on-chain pieces (address book, one-time accounts) and a command-line client. What we reuse: Solana's own confidential balances and proof system. Not building: a wallet, a web app, or our own cryptography primitives.
